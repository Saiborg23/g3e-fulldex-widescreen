**Known limits**
- Rain is a bit thinner at 16:9 (same number of drops over a wider area).

- Full-screen menus (bag, Pokédex) stay 3:2, as in FireRed.
- Fulldex's built-in patch covers v1.0.22 only. Each new release needs it regenerated.
